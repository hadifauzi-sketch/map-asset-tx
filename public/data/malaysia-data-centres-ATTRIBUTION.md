Changes: source phase records are grouped by campus, fields are normalised for the app, and status colours and presentation are adapted to the app's theme. Public anonymous campus identifiers, source net capacity, mapping eligibility, coordinates, precision and check dates are retained. Campuses are not identified or merged with named Ringmast4r facilities by proximity.

This snapshot is independent of the Ringmast4r directory. The Malaysia tracker provides campus/phase research and mapped locations that the global directory does not necessarily contain. Net MW represents built or contracted capacity on the published basis, not electricity use. Campus build-out rows count only capacity beyond listed phases.
